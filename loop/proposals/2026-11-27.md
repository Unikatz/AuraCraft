## Items

### 1. Wood Door, step 1 of 2: ship the block, not placeable yet

- Source: feedback issue 2 (8 Nov, phone, rated 2 of 5: asks for doors in other materials). Owner
  decision 14 Nov, option b: one Wood Door in a later week, after the rollback test. The rollback
  test (R1) was done and reported on 16 Nov, and R2 (Mystery Block) shipped on 24 Nov, so the
  owner's condition is met. `DECISIONS.md` still lists it as "On hold until the rollback test is
  reported".
- Problem: the only door is the Glass Rock door. The rollback rule says a new block must first ship
  as not placeable, so that `stable` already knows it before any world can hold it.
- Proposed change: add two new block ids at the end of the list, Wood Door and Open Wood Door, both
  `noplace:true` in this release. Same one-press, two-high open and close as the glass door (the
  code at the glass-door toggle is made to handle both pairs). Wood look in the game's own soft
  style. No change for players yet: the door cannot be picked or placed. A later release (step 2,
  not part of this item) makes the closed Wood Door placeable, once `stable` holds step 1.
- Tests: the two ids exist and are not placeable; the open/close toggle works in the test harness on
  a placed-by-code pair (including mixed state and a third stacked block that must not move, as for
  glass); `tests/rollback.js` passes (no placeable block that `stable` lacks).
- Effort: about 1 day, tests included.
- Risk: low for players (nothing visible changes). Adds 2 block ids for good, which can never be
  removed or reused. Direction: the Approver flagged doors as "closer to Minecraft" on 13 Nov;
  the owner has since chosen this path (one Wood Door), so the dev reads it as within that decision.
- Recommendation: approve step 1 only.

### 2. Healthy play: evening wind-down note (H2)

- Source: owner backlog (16 Nov), owner decision 20 Nov (E2, option a: device-only records are
  not data collection; evening time 8 pm). Not tester feedback.
- Problem: nothing in the game nudges toward bedtime.
- Proposed change: from 8 pm on the device's own clock, one gentle note per evening: "It's getting
  late. Time to tuck Doggy and Mew into bed soon." A toast-style note, it does not pause or block
  play. "Shown today" is a date kept on the device under its own storage key
  (`aura-isles-day`), not inside the world save, so the save format and rollback are untouched.
  If the stretch break screen is up at 8 pm, the note waits until it closes.
- Tests: fake clock at 19:59 (no note) and 20:01 (note once); a reload the same evening shows no
  second note; the next evening shows it again; the world save is byte-identical with and without H2.
- Effort: about half a day.
- Risk: low. A child playing at 7:55 pm sees it 5 minutes in; that is intended.
- Recommendation: approve.

### 3. Healthy play: daily play total with a soft wrap-up (H3)

- Source: owner backlog (16 Nov), owner decision 20 Nov (E2, option a). Not tester feedback.
- Problem: nothing tells a child they have played a long time today.
- Proposed change: count today's active play minutes (same rule as the H1 timer: only while the
  world is running and not paused) in the same device-only key as H2, reset when the date changes.
  At 60 minutes, once per day: "Great building today! Time for a real-world adventure", with Keep
  playing right away (no countdown, nothing locked). If the stretch break is on screen at that
  moment (30 and 60 minutes can line up), the wrap-up shows after the break closes, never on top.
  The count is shown to no one and sent nowhere.
- Tests: fake clock to 59 and 61 minutes (shown once); paused time not counted; date change resets;
  break and wrap-up never on screen together; world save unchanged.
- Effort: about half a day (shares the storage and tests with H2).
- Risk: low. The 60-minute figure is the dev's first pick from the backlog; no parent setting in this
  slice.
- Recommendation: approve, as one build with H2.

### 4. "Tell us" sends the device type, which the owner's list does not include

- Source: dev review, from the Approver's 24 Nov release note. Not tester feedback.
- Problem: the charter's standing owner decision says the "Tell us" button sends only a pick, a
  preset reason, the game version and the isle. The live game also sends `device` ("Touch screen"
  or "Computer"). This has been live since 12 Nov. It identifies no one, but it is information
  about the player sent off the device that the owner did not list.
- Proposed change: either (a) stop sending `device` (one field, about 30 minutes with a test that
  checks the exact fields sent), or (b) keep it and the owner adds "touch screen or computer" to
  the list. The dev cannot choose: it is the owner's list.
- Effort: (a) about 30 minutes. (b) none.
- Risk: (a) very low; we lose the phone/computer hint on "Tell us" notes (the feedback form still
  asks for device). (b) none in code.
- Recommendation: send to the owner, with (a) suggested as the default that matches the written
  rule.
